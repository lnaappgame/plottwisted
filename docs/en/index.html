2. What data is processed?

2.1 Local game data (by default)

Plot Twist(ed) does not ask for an account, a name or an email address to play. By default, your progress (unlocked worlds, jokers, display and accessibility settings, chosen avatar, automatically generated player ID such as “Cinephile #123456”) is stored only on your device (the app's local storage) and is not sent to any server, unless you yourself turn on one of the features described in sections 2.2 and 2.3 below. If you uninstall the app or use the “Reset save data” option, this local data is deleted from your device.

2.2 Backup and restore linked to a Google account (optional)

If you choose to turn on the “Save with Google” button in the settings, the app uses the Firebase Authentication and Firebase Firestore services (provided by Google) as follows:

  • From its first launch, each installation of the app is given an anonymous technical identifier (an “anonymous” Firebase account), without any action on your part.
  • When you turn on Google backup, this anonymous identifier is linked to your Google account through the Google Sign-In service. In this context, Google sends us the email address of that account, for the sole purpose of recognizing the account if you sign in again later from another device (so that we can offer to restore your progress). This email address is never shown to other players, and LNA App never uses it for communication or marketing.
  • All of your local game data (progress, settings, avatar, player ID) is then copied to Google's Firestore servers, in a technical document associated with this identifier, so that it can be restored on another device or after a reinstall.

This feature is strictly optional: if you never turn it on, none of this data is sent and only what is described in section 2.1 applies.

2.3 Online leaderboards and Multiplayer (Firestore)

The Weekly Puzzle and Multiplayer modes rely on a leaderboard or a comparison of performances shared by several players, which requires sending some data to our Firebase Firestore servers, associated only with the anonymous technical identifier mentioned in section 2.2 (never with your name or email address, even if you have also linked a Google account):

  • for the Weekly Puzzle: your solving time, the day you solved it, and your ranking relative to the other players of the current week;
  • for Multiplayer: the result of each round played (time, win or loss), used to set the reference time offered to other players and to calculate your rating (“Elo”).

Both features require an active internet connection. Past weekly leaderboards are not automatically deleted from our servers when the next week replaces them; they simply stop being actively displayed in the app (see section 6).

2.4 Analytics and technical diagnostics (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics)

The app uses Firebase Analytics to measure general usage of the app (for example: which game mode is started, a level completed, an ad watched, a purchase made) and Firebase Crashlytics to receive an automatic technical report when the app crashes (type of error, technical state of the app at the time of the incident). These tools, provided by Google, collect technical data about your device (model, operating system version, installation identifier) but are not intended to identify you personally.

2.5 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads (rewarded videos and ads between levels) provided by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and personalize these ads, Google may collect technical data about your device: advertising identifier (AAID on Android, IDFA on iOS — the latter only being accessible after your explicit permission through Apple's “App Tracking Transparency” prompt), approximate IP address, device information and interactions with ads.

LNA App does not receive or store this data itself: it is processed directly by Google, under its own privacy policy, available here: policies.google.com/privacy

A consent tool (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown to users located in the European Economic Area before any personalized ad is displayed, in accordance with the GDPR.

2.6 In-app purchases (Google Play / App Store)

Plot Twist(ed) offers optional in-app purchases (joker packs, ad removal — full list in the Terms of Use). These purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or Apple's App Store: LNA App never sees or keeps your payment information (card number, etc.). Only the confirmation that a purchase was made is sent to the app, in order to unlock the corresponding content.

2.7 Fonts (Google Fonts)

The app loads some fonts from Google's servers on first launch. This technical request does not send any personal data beyond what is strictly necessary to deliver the font file (IP address, processed directly by Google).

2.8 What we do not do

LNA App does not sell any data to third parties, does not use any analytics or ad-targeting tool other than those listed above (Google AdMob, Firebase), does not use the data described in sections 2.2 to 2.4 for any purpose other than the one it was collected for, and has no access to your contacts, photos, precise location or any other data on your device not mentioned in this policy.

5. Description of the Service

Plot Twist(ed) is a movie guessing game in which the User finds the title of a movie from a pitch whose character names have been deliberately swapped, with the help of clues and jokers. The App is available in French and English.

The App offers in particular the following modes:

  • Main game: a progression through themed worlds, each grouping several puzzles of increasing difficulty.
  • Daily Challenge: a timed daily challenge, identical for all players on the same day, with tracking of the personal best time.
  • Weekly Puzzle: a weekly puzzle shared by all players, whose text is gradually revealed, with an online leaderboard (see section 9).
  • Multiplayer: rounds played against a reference time (a real opponent recorded earlier, or a calibration time when real data is insufficient), with a rating system (“Elo”) and a match history.

The App offers help jokers obtained through progress, by watching optional ads or by purchase, as well as optional In-App Purchases described in section 11.

This description is given for information only; the Publisher reserves the right to change, add or remove features under the conditions of section 20.

9. Online features: leaderboards and multiplayer

Weekly Puzzle. The User's solving time and their ranking relative to the other players of the same week are sent to and kept on the Publisher's Firebase servers, associated with an anonymous technical identifier (and not with a personal identity), in order to calculate and display a shared leaderboard. This leaderboard is reset every week; a personal history of past results remains available locally on the User's device.

Multiplayer. This mode does not connect two players in real time: it compares the User's performance with a reference time, which may come from a game actually recorded by another player at a different time, or from a generic calibration time when the real data available for a given puzzle is insufficient. The result of each round played by the User (time, win/loss) is kept on the Publisher's servers to feed this mechanism for the benefit of all players, anonymously.

The User undertakes not to artificially manipulate these mechanisms (automation, exploitation of technical flaws) in order to distort a leaderboard or obtain an undue advantage; the Publisher reserves the right to remove from a leaderboard any result obviously obtained fraudulently.

These features require an active internet connection and are not available offline. The number of daily Multiplayer games may be limited, with the option of getting extra games by watching optional ads.
